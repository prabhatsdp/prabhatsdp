# 👋 Hi, I’m Prabhat Pandey

I’m a **Tech Lead - Mobile** at [FiatPe](https://fiatpe.com), where I help build reliable, secure, and scalable **fintech mobile products** used by businesses and merchants.

I have around **6 years of experience** in Android and cross-platform development, working across **mobile apps, POS apps, payment SDKs, OTT apps, live streaming apps, and fintech platforms**.

My focus is simple:

> Build mobile products that are fast, stable, maintainable, and useful enough that users don’t need to think about the engineering behind them.

I enjoy turning complex product requirements into clean mobile architecture, reducing crashes before users notice them, and making apps feel smooth even when the backend, SDKs, devices, and networks are all having their own Monday morning.

## 💡 Engineering Philosophy

I believe good mobile engineering is not only about writing code.

It is about building products that:

- solve real problems,
- stay stable in production,
- are easy to maintain,
- scale with business needs,
- and do not make the next developer question their career choices.

Clean code matters. User experience matters. Stability matters.
Because “it works on my machine” is not a product strategy.
